Disc-OS Portuguese Asterisk sound prompt notice
================================================

This image may include the Portuguese wake-up prompt `this-is-yr-wakeup-call`
from the Disc-OS Sounds 1.0 pt_BR package.

Project / attribution: Disc-OS Team
Source archive:
https://sourceforge.net/projects/disc-os/files/Disc-OS%20Sounds/1.0-RELEASE/Disc-OS-Sounds-1.0-pt_BR.tar.gz/download
Project page: https://disc-os.org/

The Disc-OS project states that its Portuguese voice prompts are released under
a Creative Commons 2.5 Brazil license. This add-on uses the prompt as an
Asterisk wake-up-call recording and does not sell or relicense the recording.

If the Disc-OS archive is unavailable during image build, the add-on does not
copy the Portuguese recording and falls back to Asterisk's standard English
`this-is-yr-wakeup-call` Extra Sounds prompt, and finally to `beep` if neither
recording is available.
